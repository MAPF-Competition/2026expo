## DeepFleet: Multi-Agent Foundation Models for Mobile Robots

**Bio:** Ameya Agaskar is a senior research scientist at Amazon Robotics working on foundation models for fleets of mobile robots. His prior research experience includes work on RF systems at MIT Lincoln Laboratory, graph signal processing work (for which he received a PhD) at Harvard University, audio watermarking for Amazon Alexa devices, and audio foundation models in Amazon’s AGI division.
